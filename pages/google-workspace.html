Storage Architecture

How Google Workspace Pooled Storage Works

Google Workspace uses a unified organizational pooled storage model, meaning total storage is combined across all user licenses and can be centrally allocated by administrators.

📦

10 Business Starter Users

10 × 30 GB = 300 GB

The company receives a total pool of 300 GB shared across Gmail, Google Drive files, and shared team assets.

🚀

10 Business Standard Users

10 × 2 TB = 20 TB

The company receives a massive 20,000 GB (20 TB) total pooled storage pool for seamless video, media, and document archives.

🏢

20 Business Plus Users

20 × 5 TB = 100 TB

The company receives an enterprise-grade 100 TB pooled storage capacity with Google Vault compliance retention.

Key Advantages of Pooled Storage:

  • No Individual User Blockers: If one employee uses 50 GB and another uses only 5 GB, the company pool covers both seamlessly without requiring individual quota upgrades.
  • Unified Gmail & Drive Storage: Pooled storage applies simultaneously across all user Gmail inboxes, Google Drive folders, and Shared Drives.
  • Administrator Quota Controls: Workspace administrators can easily configure custom per-user storage caps or departmental limits from the Google Admin Console.

Frequently Asked Questions

Google Workspace FAQs

Answers to common questions about Google Workspace licensing, custom business email, migration, and partner support.

What is Google Workspace for Business?

Google Workspace is Google's integrated suite of cloud-based productivity, communication, collaboration, and administration tools designed for businesses. It includes custom domain business email with Gmail, cloud storage on Google Drive, Google Meet video conferencing, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Chat, and Gemini AI features.

Why should we buy Google Workspace through BHAR India instead of directly from Google?

Purchasing through BHAR India gives you the exact same official Google cloud infrastructure at competitive partner rates, along with complimentary technical setup (DNS, MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC), painless data migration from your current email host, GST input tax invoicing, and direct human technical support in India without automated queue delays.

Can we use our existing company domain name for business email?

Yes. You keep your domain name (e.g., yourcompany.com), and we configure the DNS and mail exchange records so every team member receives a branded professional email address (e.g., yourname@yourcompany.com) powered by the secure Gmail engine.

Can we migrate existing emails, contacts, and calendar entries from our old provider?

Yes. BHAR India provides full email migration services from Microsoft 365, Exchange, Outlook, cPanel webmail, GoDaddy, Rediffmail, and standard IMAP servers, ensuring your historical communications are safely preserved.

How does pooled storage work across Google Workspace user licenses?

Google Workspace storage is pooled at the organization level. For example, 10 Business Standard licenses generate 20 TB of pooled storage (10 users × 2 TB) that is shared dynamically across all mailboxes, Drive files, and Shared Drives.

What is the difference between Business Starter, Business Standard, and Business Plus?

Business Starter offers 30 GB pooled storage/user and 100-person video meetings. Business Standard upgrades storage to 2 TB/user, allows 150-person meetings with Drive recording, noise cancellation, and enhanced Gemini AI. Business Plus provides 5 TB/user, 500-person meetings with attendance tracking, Google Vault for legal retention/eDiscovery, and advanced endpoint management.

Can different employees in the same company have different Google Workspace plans?

Depending on organizational size and Google licensing policy agreements, mixed licensing (e.g., standard users on Business Starter and executives/legal on Business Standard or Plus) can be structured to optimize overall corporate software spend.

What is Google Vault and which businesses need it?

Google Vault is an information governance and eDiscovery tool available on Business Plus and Enterprise plans. It retains emails, chats, and files for specified audit periods, supports litigation holds, and enables searchable exports for legal compliance in banking, healthcare, and regulated industries.

How does Gemini AI integrate into Google Workspace apps?

Gemini AI is built directly into Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet to draft responses, summarize documents and email threads, assist with data formula creation, generate slides, and capture meeting action items based on edition capabilities.

How quickly can our organization get set up with Google Workspace?

New Google Workspace setups and domain verifications are typically completed within 2 to 4 hours. Email migrations depend on mailbox sizes and are scheduled with zero operational disruption to active communications.
