BHAR India helps organizations automate repetitive work across ERP systems, finance operations, customer workflows, reports, and document-heavy processes.

RPA Services

What this service covers

Our robotic process automation service is designed for businesses that spend too much time on repetitive, rules-based work. We study the existing process, identify handoffs and exceptions, build the automation workflow, test it with your users, and support the rollout so the bot performs reliably in real operations.

Typical use cases

Invoice processing, report generation, ERP data entry, reconciliation steps, and portal updates.

Industries served

Finance, insurance, real estate, retail, manufacturing, education, and government-linked workflows.

Technologies used

UiPath, OCR-based extraction, spreadsheet workflows, email triggers, APIs, and ERP interfaces.

Why businesses choose RPA

To reduce manual effort, improve consistency, shorten turnaround times, and keep audit trails clearer.

Direct Answers

How BHAR India approaches RPA delivery

The goal is not to automate every task blindly. We focus on stable processes, clear exception paths, and business outcomes your team can actually use.

What problems does RPA solve?

It helps where teams repeat the same steps across multiple systems, spend time on copy-paste work, chase files in email, or depend on manual report preparation. Good automations reduce avoidable delays and repetitive errors.

Who needs this service?

Operations, finance, procurement, HR, sales support, claims, and back-office teams that process high volumes of routine transactions are strong candidates for RPA.

How does implementation work?

We begin with a process review, map business rules and exceptions, identify integrations, build the workflow, test it with real scenarios, and support the go-live with monitoring and change handling.

What integrations are possible?

Existing ERP systems, CRM tools, websites, Windows applications, Excel files, shared folders, emails, OCR workflows, and APIs can all be part of the solution depending on your process.

Can the automation be customized?

Yes. Business rules, approvals, retries, validations, escalation conditions, and outputs can all be adjusted to match the process your team actually follows.

What should a business consider first?

Before choosing RPA, the business should review process stability, exception frequency, input quality, user ownership, and whether the workflow is best solved through UI automation, API integration, or both.

FAQ

Common questions about RPA services

What is robotic process automation?

Robotic process automation uses software bots to complete repetitive rule-based work across business systems, such as data entry, document processing, status checks, report downloads, and update workflows.

Which processes are suitable for RPA?

RPA works best for high-volume, rule-based tasks such as invoice handling, report generation, ERP updates, email-driven workflows, reconciliation steps, and repetitive portal operations.

Can RPA work with existing ERP or CRM software?

Yes. BHAR India can automate workflows around systems such as SAP, Tally, Oracle, CRM tools, web portals, spreadsheets, email, and OCR-based document inputs without replacing the existing platform.

How long does an RPA implementation take?

Implementation timelines depend on process complexity, exception handling, integrations, and testing scope. Smaller automations move faster, while broader multi-step processes require deeper discovery and user acceptance testing.
