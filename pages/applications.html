Frequently Asked Questions

Business Applications FAQs

Common questions about choosing, customizing, and deploying BHAR India's business applications.

What types of business applications does BHAR India provide?

BHAR India builds ready-to-deploy and custom business applications for diverse operational workflows, including CRM & Lead Management, Clinic Management Systems with EMR, POS + Captain restaurant systems, Retail POS barcode billing, Asset Management Systems, and Property Dealer Management Systems.

How do BHAR India's business applications compare to generic SaaS subscriptions?

Generic SaaS subscriptions charge recurring per-user monthly fees, enforce rigid workflows, and store your data on shared multi-tenant clouds. BHAR India provides tailored software with complete data ownership, customizable modules, lifetime cost efficiency, and seamless local integrations.

Can these applications be customized for our specific business processes?

Yes. Every application module—including custom fields, user roles, approval hierarchies, receipt formats, tax structures, and reporting dashboards—can be customized to align precisely with your existing operations.

How does the CRM & Lead Management System improve conversion rates?

The CRM automatically captures leads from websites, WhatsApp, and ad campaigns, assigns them via round-robin rules, tracks follow-up reminders, visualizes deal pipelines, and provides conversion analytics so sales teams never miss an opportunity.

What healthcare and clinical features are included in the Clinic Management System?

It includes patient registration, appointment scheduling, token queues, digital prescription generation with dosage templates, Electronic Medical Records (EMR), lab report attachments, and GST-compliant consultation billing.

How do the Retail POS and POS + Captain systems accelerate checkout and billing?

Our Retail POS enables 5-second barcode billing, real-time stock deduction, low-stock alerts, and multi-tender split payments (Cash, UPI QR, Cards). The POS + Captain system lets restaurant waitstaff take table orders on mobile devices, dispatching instant digital KOTs directly to kitchen printers.

How does the Asset Management System track physical hardware and IT assets?

It manages the complete asset lifecycle—from QR/barcode asset tagging and employee assignments to preventive maintenance scheduling, depreciation calculations (SLM/WDV), and employee exit handovers.

How does the Property Dealer Management System manage real estate listings and buyer leads?

It centralizes residential and commercial listings, matches buyer requirements with available inventory, tracks agent site visits with client feedback, and manages unit bookings and commission splits.

Do the applications support role-based access control, security, and audit logs?

Yes. All applications implement strict role-based access control (RBAC), end-to-end data encryption, session management, and detailed audit trails recording who created, edited, or deleted records.

Where can I read detailed project blogs, implementation guides, and case studies for each application?

You can read dedicated project blogs with in-depth architecture and workflow breakdowns for each application: CRM System, Clinic Management, POS + Captain, Retail POS, Asset Management, and Property Dealer System on our blog portal.
