Frequently Asked Questions

Workflow Automation FAQs

Common questions about automating enterprise processes across SAP, tax, accounting, and customer operations.

What enterprise process automation and RPA solutions does BHAR India deliver?

BHAR India provides end-to-end intelligent automation solutions including SAP Report Generation, Smart SAP Process Automation, GST Calculation and GSTR-2B Reconciliation, Intelligent Invoice Processing with AI-OCR, Sales to ERP Workflow Synchronization, and E-KYC Government Portal Automation.

How does SAP Report Generation Automation eliminate repetitive manual reporting?

It automatically executes scheduled SAP transactions (FBL1N, MB52, VA05, custom Z-reports), applies dynamic parameters, extracts raw datasets, compiles branded Excel dashboards with pivot tables, and distributes reports to managers without manual intervention.

How does Intelligent Invoice Processing with AI-OCR streamline Accounts Payable (AP)?

The solution extracts invoice data from vendor PDFs or scans using AI-OCR, validates supplier GSTINs and line items, executes 3-way matching against Purchase Orders (PO) and Goods Receipts (GRN), and posts entries directly into your ERP.

How does GST Calculation Automation assist with GSTR-2B reconciliation and ITC matching?

It automatically verifies CGST/SGST vs IGST applicability, validates HSN/SAC codes against statutory CBIC tax rates, reconciles purchase registers with monthly GSTR-2B portal data, and flags ineligible or missing ITC.

How does Smart SAP Process Automation handle bulk Excel uploads and transaction postings?

Software bots read structured Excel spreadsheets, validate business rules, navigate SAP GUI screens, enter transaction values, and capture created SAP document numbers, saving hundreds of manual data entry hours.

How does Sales to ERP Workflow Automation eliminate duplicate order transcription?

It captures confirmed sales orders from CRMs, e-commerce stores, dealer portals, or EDI, checks real-time inventory and customer credit limits, translates external part numbers to ERP material numbers, and creates sales orders (VA01) in ERP automatically.

How does E-KYC Government Portal Automation accelerate digital identity verification?

It uses AI vision OCR to extract details from Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, and Voter IDs, verifies documents against authorized government verification registries in real time, and maintains tamper-evident audit dossiers.

Do these automation bots require modifying core SAP ABAP code or ERP database schemas?

No. The automations run on standard SAP GUI scripting, RFCs, BAPIs, or secure REST APIs using authorized role permissions, requiring zero invasive code modifications to your production ERP.

What data encryption, credential management, and security standards protect enterprise automation workflows?

Automations employ enterprise credential vaults (Windows Credential Manager, CyberArk), TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, AES-256 at rest, role-based permissions, and comprehensive transaction logs for audit compliance.
