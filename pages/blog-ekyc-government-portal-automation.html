E-KYC Government Portal Automation for Faster Customer Onboarding

By BHAR TeamJuly 11, 20268 min read
Innovate . Automate . Elevate

Customer onboarding slows down when identity documents are checked manually. E-KYC automation helps capture documents, extract data, validate details, initiate permitted verification, and store audit-ready records.

Digital Document Verification

The system can process supported documents such as Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence, voter ID, passport, and DigiLocker documents based on enabled integrations and business eligibility.

Accuracy And Security

OCR extracts names, dates of birth, document numbers, addresses, and expiry dates. The system can compare extracted details with customer-entered data and flag mismatches, duplicates, poor document quality, or unsupported documents.

Important Controls

  • Consent capture and authentication
  • Role-based access to sensitive documents
  • Masked identity numbers where required
  • Retry support for unavailable verification services
  • Audit logs for upload, review, approval, and rejection

Onboarding Benefits

E-KYC automation reduces paperwork, manual verification effort, duplicate applications, and processing delays. It improves customer experience while giving compliance teams better visibility.
